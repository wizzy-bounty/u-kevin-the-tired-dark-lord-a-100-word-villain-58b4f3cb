A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — the Lich Accountant of the Barrowlands, nine centuries on a cursed throne, who will raise the dead just as soon as he finishes the ledgers.

> Listen. I have ruled the Barrowlands for nine centuries, and tonight — tonight — the dead will rise. They will rise... after I finish these ledgers. Do you know how many souls crossed the river last quarter? Forty-one thousand. Each one needs a name, a cause, a signature. My throne — this magnificent, cursed throne — has a loose nail that has been stabbing my left cheek for two hundred years. The eclipse is coming. The eclipse can wait five minutes. I just need to sit here. Just — five minutes. The dead are patient. I taught them that.
